How this world is made

01
One seed, one world

The seed initializes every random decision. Share it to reproduce this exact terrain.

02
Layered noise

Five octaves of smooth value noise form the elevation, moisture, and temperature maps.

03
Infinite chunks

The map streams 16 × 16 tile chunks around you as you move with W, A, S, and D.

04
Climate-driven biomes

Altitude, temperature, and moisture decide where forests, deserts, snow, and objects appear.

05
Adaptive detail

Distant terrain is sampled at lower resolution to keep exploration smooth.

06
Mesh export
